## Why go

The imperial monastery of the Komnenoi — three linked churches built in the
1120s–30s by the Empress Irene and John II, the burial place of the
Komnenian and early Palaiologan emperors, and the site of the great
Pantokrator hospital, whose founding typikon is one of the most important
documents of Byzantine social history. The largest surviving Byzantine
religious complex in the city after Hagia Sophia.

## Practical notes

Between the Aqueduct of Valens and the Golden Horn, so it falls on the axis
between Sultanahmet and Fener. The interior decoration is largely gone; the
*opus sectile* floor is the thing to look for.
