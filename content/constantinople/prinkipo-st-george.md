## Why go

The great popular pilgrimage of the Constantinopolitan Greeks: a monastery
on the summit of Prinkipo, reached on St George's day by a steep climb that
pilgrims make barefoot, unwinding a thread up the hill as they go, and
attended in enormous numbers by Orthodox and Muslims alike. The island also
holds the Monastery of Christ, with the ruins of the Halki Theological
School on neighbouring Heybeliada.

## Practical notes

**This needs its own day, and will not fit around anything else.** Ferries
from Kabataş or Eminönü take an hour or more each way and run to a fixed
timetable, and the climb up from the Büyükada quay is another forty minutes
on foot. On St George's day it is one of the great popular pilgrimages of
the Orthodox world, and worth planning a visit to the city around.
