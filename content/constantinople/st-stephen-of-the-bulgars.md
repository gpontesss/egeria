## Why go

The "iron church" — a complete neo-Byzantine church prefabricated in cast
iron in Vienna in the 1890s, shipped down the Danube and up the Bosphorus
in sections and bolted together on the shore of the Golden Horn, built for
the Bulgarian community during the struggle over the Bulgarian Exarchate.
Restored and regilded in 2018, and unlike almost everything else in Balat
it is a functioning Orthodox church you can simply walk into.

## Practical notes

On the shore road in Balat, a few minutes from the Patriarchate — a natural
addition to the Fener cluster.
