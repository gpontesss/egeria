## Why go

**The seat of the Ecumenical Patriarchate from 1456 to 1587** — the church
the Patriarchate used for over a century after the loss of Hagia Sophia,
and before the move to the Phanar. Its fourteenth-century funerary chapel
(*parekklesion*) holds mosaics second only to Chora's: a Pantokrator with
the prophets in the dome, a Deësis, and a Baptism.

## Practical notes

A short walk up the hill from the Patriarchate, so it belongs with the
Fener cluster. The mosque and the museum chapel are entered separately.
