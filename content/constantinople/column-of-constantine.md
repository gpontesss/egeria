## Why go

Constantine's own column, raised in 330 at the dedication of the city, at
the centre of his forum — the relics said to have been buried at its base
(among them, in the later tradition, fragments of the True Cross and the
crosses of the thieves) made it the ritual centre of Christian
Constantinople. Burnt, banded with iron, and still standing.

Along the same axis, all free and all in the street: the **Milion**, the
milestone from which the roads of the Empire were measured; the
**Hippodrome** with the Serpent Column from Delphi and the Obelisk of
Theodosius; and the **Aqueduct of Valens** further west.

## Practical notes

Five-minute stops, all of them passed anyway on the walk between
Sultanahmet and the bazaar: no ticket, no queue, no hours.
