## Why go

One of the earliest churches of the City, rebuilt by Justinian after the
Nika riots, and **the church in which the Second Ecumenical Council met in
381** — the council that completed the Creed. Never converted to a mosque;
it stood inside the palace precinct and was used as an arsenal, which is
why it survives with its iconoclast-period apse mosaic of a bare cross, one
of the very few pieces of iconoclastic decoration left anywhere.
