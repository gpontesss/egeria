## Why go

Traditionally identified with the church of **St Theodosia**, the nun
martyred in 729 for defending the icon of Christ over the Chalke gate
against the iconoclasts — the first martyr of the iconoclast persecution.
Her shrine was one of the busiest pilgrimages of the late City, and the
Turkish name *Gül Camii*, "the mosque of roses", is said to come from the
flowers heaped on it on her feast, which fell on 29 May 1453 — the day the
City fell.

## Practical notes

On the Golden Horn shore road between Fener and the Pantokrator; a short
detour on that axis.
