## Why go

The Studion — founded in 462, the most influential monastery in the history
of Orthodox monasticism. Its typikon under **St Theodore the Studite**
shaped cenobitic life and the liturgical books across the Orthodox world;
the Triodion and much of the structure of the services as they are
celebrated today come out of this house. The oldest surviving church
building in Istanbul.

What survives is a roofless basilica shell with its colonnade and floor.

## Practical notes

Near the land walls at the southern end, not far from the **Golden Gate**
and the Theodosian walls. Access has been closed or restricted for
years; treat a visit as unlikely, and check anyway.
