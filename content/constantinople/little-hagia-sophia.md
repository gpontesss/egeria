## Why go

"Little Hagia Sophia" — built by Justinian and Theodora in the 530s, just
before the Great Church, and the architectural experiment that made Hagia
Sophia possible: the same problem of a dome over a centralised plan, solved
at a scale you can take in at a glance. The dedicatory inscription to
Justinian and Theodora still runs around the entablature, and the carved
capitals are superb.

## Practical notes

Ten minutes' walk downhill from the Hippodrome, in a quiet neighbourhood,
with a tea garden in the courtyard. The least crowded great building in the
city.
