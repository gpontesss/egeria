## Why go

Founded in 1713 by the monk Makarios Kalogeras, this is one of the oldest
continuously operating Greek schools, and through the eighteenth century it
was among the most important centres of Greek learning under Ottoman rule —
the "Patmias" educated clergy and teachers for the whole Aegean. It still
functions as an ecclesiastical school.

## Practical notes

It sits immediately above the Cave of the Apocalypse and is passed on the
way; whether you can go in depends on the school term and on asking.
