## Why go

A great monolith standing at the edge of the sea, honeycombed with
hand-cut cells, cisterns, niches and stairs — a hermitage complex used by
ascetics from the Byzantine period onwards, abandoned and open. There is
nothing interpretive and no custodian; you climb it and look at what
somebody carved out of the rock to pray in.

## Practical notes

Twenty minutes' drive, or a long walk, south from Skala at Grikos. Nothing
to venerate: this is for the visitor who wants the eremitic side of the
island rather than the monastic one.
