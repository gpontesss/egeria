## Why go

Patmos has a continuing eremitic life alongside its two great houses, in
small skites and cells scattered over the island — some dependencies of the
Monastery of St John, some private, most not open to visitors and not
marked.

## Practical notes

**This entry is a pointer, not a list.** Which hermitages can be visited
changes with who is living in them, and a published list would be both wrong
and intrusive. Ask at the Monastery of St John — ideally with a letter of
introduction from your own parish priest — rather than hunting for cells on
a map.
