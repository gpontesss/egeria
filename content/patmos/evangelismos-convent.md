## Why go

A working convent of nuns above Chora, founded in the twentieth century on
an older site, known for its iconography and ecclesiastical embroidery —
the nuns' workshop is one of the serious icon-painting workshops of the
Dodecanese. The church, the garden and the view down both coasts of the
island are the visit.

## Practical notes

The opening window is narrow and the walk out from Chora is twenty-five
minutes along the ridge. Confirm the hour in Chora before setting out —
arriving after it closes is a wasted hour each way.
