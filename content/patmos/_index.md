The whole island is a declared holy place, and its two principal sites — the
Cave and the Monastery — are a steep fifteen-minute walk apart on the same
hillside above the port. Everything else on Patmos is an addition to those
two: convents that admit visitors for part of the morning, a working
ecclesiastical school, and hermitages that take a hike to reach.

Patmos has no airport, which keeps it quieter than its importance would
suggest and makes it the hardest place here to reach.
