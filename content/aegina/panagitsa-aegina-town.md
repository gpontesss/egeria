## Why go

The small blue-domed Panagitsa on the harbour mole is the first thing
visible from the boat and the last thing on leaving, and the town's churches
— the cathedral, St Nicholas Thalassinos on the quay, and the chapels in the
back streets — make a half-hour walk while waiting for a ferry. Aegina town
was the first capital of the Greek state in 1828–29, and several of its
churches carry that history.
