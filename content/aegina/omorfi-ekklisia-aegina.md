## Why go

A small thirteenth-century single-aisled church built of ancient spolia
from the nearby temple sites, with a frescoed interior — the best surviving
medieval wall painting on the island outside Palaiochora. "The beautiful
church" is what the islanders have always called it.

## Practical notes

Directly on the Palaiochora–St Nektarios road, so it costs nothing to
attempt; the frescoes are behind a locked door more often than not.
