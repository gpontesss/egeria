Aegina is the shortest pilgrimage in the Saronic Gulf and the densest:
forty minutes by hydrofoil or seventy by ferry from Piraeus, with the two
places that matter a short drive apart in the island's interior.

It can be done between boats. An overnight buys the Liturgy at St Nektarios
before the first day-trippers land, which is a different island.
