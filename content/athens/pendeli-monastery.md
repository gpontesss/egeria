## Why go

A large living monastery on the pine slopes of Mount Pendeli, founded in
1578, with a sixteenth-century katholikon, later chapels, a monastic
museum, and the eccentric nineteenth-century "Duchess of Plaisance" cave
chapel on the grounds. The most accessible functioning monastery of any
size near Athens.

## Practical notes

Half a day including transport. Reachable by bus from the northern suburbs,
or a 25-minute taxi from the centre.
