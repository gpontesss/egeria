## Why go

The largest surviving medieval building in Athens: an eleventh-century
domed octagon, the same ambitious plan as Daphni, built as the katholikon
of a monastery. Ceded to Russia in the nineteenth century after the
Revolution left it ruined, restored at Russian expense, and in use as the
Russian parish church of Athens ever since — so the interior is
nineteenth-century Russian inside an eleventh-century Greek shell, with a
tall detached bell tower added at the same time.

## Practical notes

Three minutes' walk from Syntagma square, and easy to fold into any day.
