## Why go

The best collection of Byzantine and post-Byzantine icons, liturgical
vessels, vestments, manuscripts and architectural sculpture in Greece,
arranged historically — including whole fresco cycles lifted from
destroyed churches and reassembled, and a reconstructed Early Christian
basilica. For understanding what you are looking at in the churches, this
is the single most useful afternoon in Athens.

## Practical notes

Next door to the Benaki Museum, which has its own strong icon collection
and a good roof café; the two make one day. A museum, not a shrine: there is
nothing here for veneration.
