## Why go

A twelfth-century katholikon with a later exonarthex high on Hymettus, with
views over the whole Attic basin. For anyone who wants a walk on the
mountain with a Byzantine church at the end of it. Pairs with Kaisariani,
lower on the same slope.

## Practical notes

Walking shoes and water; there is no shade on the approach and no facilities.
