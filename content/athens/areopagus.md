## Why go

St Paul stood here and preached the sermon in Acts 17 — the "Unknown God"
sermon, delivered to the council of the Areopagus with the temples of the
Acropolis directly above him. The first Athenian convert named in the text,
Dionysius the Areopagite, was a member of that council and became the
city's first bishop.

A bronze plaque at the foot of the rock carries the full text of the sermon
in Greek. The hill itself is bare stone; there is nothing built on it and
nothing to venerate. It is a place to stand and read Acts 17 aloud, which is
in practice what pilgrims do.

## Practical notes

Thirty metres of climb from the pavement. Go at sunrise or late afternoon —
it faces the Acropolis, is unshaded, and is packed at midday. Free, and
outside the Acropolis ticket.
