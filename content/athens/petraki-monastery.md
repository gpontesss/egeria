## Why go

A living monastery inside the modern city, and the administrative centre of
the Church of Greece — the Holy Synod meets here. The katholikon is
seventeenth- and eighteenth-century on older foundations, with frescoes by
Georgios Markou of Argos, one of the significant post-Byzantine painters of
the mainland.

## Practical notes

Fifteen minutes' walk from Syntagma, at the foot of Lycabettus — a natural
first or last stop of a day in Kolonaki, paired with the Byzantine Museum.
