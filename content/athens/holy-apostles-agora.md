## Why go

Around 1000, and the oldest church in Athens still standing substantially
as built — the only building in the Ancient Agora left upright when the
site was cleared for excavation in the 1950s, precisely because it was a
church. Restored to its original tenth-century form in that clearance, with
the later additions stripped away; fragments of its frescoes survive, and
others were moved here from a demolished church nearby.

## Practical notes

Inside the ticketed Agora, a few minutes from the Stoa of Attalos and
directly below the Areopagus: the three are one walk.
