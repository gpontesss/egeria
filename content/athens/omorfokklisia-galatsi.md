## Why go

"The beautiful church" — a late twelfth- or early thirteenth-century domed
church whose interior frescoes, in several layers from the thirteenth
century on, are among the best preserved in Attica. Far less visited than
the city-centre churches because it is outside the walkable core.

## Practical notes

A short taxi from the centre. Call ahead, or accept that you may find it
locked; the frescoes are the entire reason to go, so a closed door is a real
risk.
