Athens rewards a visitor who is willing to walk. The Byzantine churches of
the old city sit within about twenty minutes of each other on foot — most of
them small, eleventh- or twelfth-century, sunk a metre or two below the
modern street — and the Cathedral holds the city's two principal relics.

The monasteries are a different day: Daphni and Kaisariani are museums with
their mosaics and frescoes intact, while Petraki and Pendeli are living
houses. The two museums on Vasilissis Sofias are the best preparation for
all of it.
