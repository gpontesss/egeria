## Why go

A well-preserved eleventh-century cross-in-square church with a
cloisonné-brick exterior and a terracotta ornamental frieze, sitting below
the modern pavement level of Klafthmonos Square. A dated inscription places
the rebuilding in the 1060s. Quiet, and usually empty.

## Practical notes

Ten minutes' walk north of Syntagma, and on the way to the Byzantine Museum
on foot.
