## Why go

A collection of Greek art from antiquity to the nineteenth century, with
strong holdings of icons, church silver, embroidered vestments and
Ottoman-era ecclesiastical objects — and good material on the Greek
communities of Asia Minor and Constantinople.

## Practical notes

Five minutes' walk from the Byzantine Museum.
